How to play

  • ✋ Look: drag to move, pinch or scroll to zoom, tap anyone or anything to inspect it.
  • 🔨 Build: pick a structure and tap the land. It costs wood (and stone); villagers do the building.
  • ⛰️ Raise / 🌊 Lower: drag to sculpt land out of the sea, or dig lakes. Two fingers still pan.
  • 🌧️ Rain waters crops and forests. 🌱 Grow plants or ripens things. 🪓 Clear removes plants and rocks.
  • Powers cost ✨ faith, which a fed, housed, happy village gives you.
  • Watch the speech bubbles: when villagers look up at you, they are asking for something.
  • Families grow when a house has two adults, a spare bed and food in the store.
